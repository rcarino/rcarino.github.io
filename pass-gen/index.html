Offline password generator

Single file, no network, no ads, no extension, no account. Randomness comes from your OS via crypto.getRandomValues() with rejection sampling (no modulo bias). Ambiguous characters (i l I L 1 o O 0) are always excluded, and at most one symbol is included, drawn from a small keyboard-friendly set. Exactly one uppercase, one digit, and at most one symbol — one shift press and one trip to the symbol keyboard; everything else is lowercase. Read the source — it's short.
